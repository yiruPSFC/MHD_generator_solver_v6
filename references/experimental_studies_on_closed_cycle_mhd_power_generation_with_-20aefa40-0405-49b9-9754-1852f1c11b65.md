#### Abstract

Experimental studies on closed cycle MHD power generation with " Fuji-1 " blow-down facility at Tokyo Institute of Technology are presented. Recently, a new disk generator (Disk-F4) has been installed and a new seed injection system has been introduced from IVTAN (Institute of High Temperature in Russia). The design concept of the new generator channel is focused mainly on the reliability of high power generation. The Mach number at the generator inlet and the thermal input are increased up to $\sim 2.8$ and $\sim 3.0 \mathrm{MW}$, respectively. In the new seeding system, a melted seed material is pushed by a piston dozator, instead of gas-pressure-driven in the previous system. The controllability of seed fraction is markedly improved, and the large fluctuation as has been observed previously is diminished. In the power generation experiments with the new components, the maximum power output of 502 kW and the enthalpy extraction ratio of $16.7 \%$ have been obtained. These values at the present stage are lower than the maximum values previously achieved in the facility. However, the reliable high power generation can be expected with the new generator. The behavior of plasma and fluid under high MHD interaction taking place in the generator channel is also discussed.


## INTRODUCTION

Studies on closed cycle MHD power generation have been conducted with Fuji-1 blow-down facility at Tokyo Institute of Technology, since 1983 (Okamura, et al., 1994). One of the main objectives in the Fuji-1 experiments is to achieve large enthalpy extraction from a disk shaped generator during several tens of seconds under low seed fractions. For this purpose, a special attention has been paid to the realization of stable and uniform non-equilibrium plasma with full seed ionization under the condition of high MHD interaction. Recently, the demonstration of a high adiabatic efficiency with high enthalpy extraction ratio has become an important subject in the studies
on closed cycle MHD power generation for the realization of plant system.

Schematic diagram of total system of the Fuji-1 facility is shown in Fig.1. Argon gas is heated up to $\sim 2000 \mathrm{~K}$ by a pebble bed type regenerative heat exchanger and cesium as a seed material is mixed with the heated gas in the hot duct. The working gas of cesium seeded argon is introduced into the disk generator, where the magnetic flux density of $\sim 4.67 \mathrm{~T}$ is applied by a Helmholtz type superconducting magnet. The working gas after passing through the generator is cooled and seed material is removed in a pebble bed cooler, and introduced into a vacuum tank equipped to get a large pressure ratio of the generator inlet to the exit.

The disk generators installed in the Fuji-1 facility are shown in Fig.2. The maximum enthalpy extraction ratio of $15.8 \%$ and the corresponding output power of 403 kW were obtained with Disk-F3a channel in 1989 under the working conditions of stagnation pressure $P_{0}=0.46 \mathrm{MPa}$, stagnation temperature $T_{0}=$ 1850 K , thermal input T.I. $=2.55 \mathrm{MW}$, seed fraction $\varepsilon=2.0 \times 10^{-4}$, load resistance $R_{L}=0.62 \Omega$ (Harada, et al., 1990). It was suggested from the Disk-F3a experiments that the increase in the ratio of generator exit area $A_{\text {exit }}$ to the inlet $A_{\text {inlet }}$ should provide high enthalpy extraction ratio because low static pressure can be maintained in the channel. On the basis of this suggestion, Disk-F3r channel as shown in Fig. 2 was introduced, where the area ratio $A_{\text {evil }} / A_{\text {die }}$ was increased up to $\sim 6.9$ from $\sim 4.1$ (Disk-F3a). With the Disk-F3r channel, the maximum enthalpy extraction ratio of $18.0 \%$ and the corresponding output power of 297 kW were achieved in 1992 under the working conditions of $P_{0}=0.42 \mathrm{MPa}, T_{\text {g } 0}=1930 \mathrm{~K}, T . I=1.65 \mathrm{MW}, \varepsilon= 3.0 \times 10^{-4}, R_{L}=0.51 \Omega$ (Harada, ${ }^{g 0}$, et al., 1993). As has been expected, the larger area ratio surely provided higher enthalpy extraction ratio. It should be noted here that the maximum enthalpy extraction ratio for Disk-F3r channel was obtained at the seed fraction of $3.0 \times 10^{-4}$ higher than that for Disk-F3a $\left(2.0 \times 10^{-4}\right)$. Thus, in a generator with a large area ratio such as

The reproducibility of high power generation with Disk-F3r, however, could not be complete. In a sense of the reliability of power generation, the Disk-F3a channel would be superior to the Disk-F3r channel. One of the reasons could be ascribed to the low Mach number (2.36) and small height at the generator inlet of Disk-F3r generator. Then, non-equilibrium plasma with a high electrical conductivity could not be realized always. Thus, properly high Mach number and a large channel height (large thermal input) should be required.

Furthermore, in the experiments with Disk-F3a and -F3r channels, the influence of the fluctuation of seed fraction on the generator performance was investigated (Harada, et al., 1993). The results showed that the large fluctuation with a low frequency of several Hz , particularly for low seed fraction $\sim 10^{-4}$, caused the fluctuation of output power with the frequency on the same order of that in the seed fraction. Then, it was pointed out that the generator performance could be markedly improved, if the fluctuation of seed fraction is suppressed.

On the basis of the suggestions above, a new disk shaped generator (Disk-F4) has been installed and a new seed injection system has been introduced from IVTAN (Institute of High Temperature in Russia) in the Fuji-1 facility. In the present paper, first of all, the design concept of the new generator Disk-F4 and the new seed injection system are described. Furthermore, the typical results of power generation experiments are shown and the problems to overcome for the improvement of generator performance will be discussed briefly.

## DISK-F4 GENERATOR CHANNEL

The design concept of the new generator channel Disk-F4 has been focused mainly on the reliability for high power generations. For this purpose, as mentioned above, the Mach number at the generator inlet and the thermal input are slightly increased up to $\sim 2.8$ and $\sim 3.0 \mathrm{MW}$ (at $P_{0}=0.42 \mathrm{MPa}, T_{0}=$ 1950 K ), respectively. As shown in Fig.2, the geometry of the Disk-F4 channel is roughly similar to that of the Disk-F3a channel which provided the well-reproducibility of power generation. The high Mach number, the large thermal input and the large inlet height (see Fig.2) should lead to produce a nonequilibrium plasma with a high electrical conductivity at the inlet. It is noted here that the thermal input and the channel height which is maximal at the generator exit are limited by the capacity of the heat exchanger and the space between the two vacuum vessels of superconducting magnet in the present Fuji1 system. The space limitation obliged the generator inlet of Disk-F3r channel to be narrow under the requirement of a large area ratio ( $A_{\text {exi }} / A_{\text {inilef }}$ ) for a high enthalpy extraction. This resulted in the fact described before. On the contrary, since the height at the inlet of Disk-F4 channel is enlarged, the area ratio is decreased to - 5.0 from the same reason. According to the suggestions from Disk-F3r experiments mentioned, the high enthalpy extraction ratio might not be obtained in comparison with the value achieved in the Disk-F3r channel, because of the rather small area ratio of Disk-F4 channel. Nevertheless, the possible plasma production with high electron temperature at the generator inlet of Disk-F4 channel should improve the generator performance with well-reproducibility. Thus, a high enthalpy extraction ratio leading to a large output power can be expected, which will probably give a high adiabatic efficiency.

The Disk-F4 generator, of which the construction is almost
the same as the Disk-F3a and -F3r channels (Okamura, et al., 1994) consists of a supersonic nozzle ( $r<140 \mathrm{~mm}$ ), MHD channel ( $170 \mathrm{~mm}<r<406 \mathrm{~mm}$ ) and a diffuser ( $r>441 \mathrm{~mm}$ ). Two ring-typed anodes, first (A1) and second anode (A2) and two cathodes, first (C1) and second cathode (C2) are put on each disk wall. The external load resistance is connected between the second anode (A2) and the second cathode (C2). The insulator walls between electrodes are made of $\mathrm{Si}_{3} \mathrm{~N}_{4}$ having high mechanical strength. Electric probes, static pressure taps and optical windows are equipped for measurements.

## IMPROVED SEED INJECTION SYSTEM

In the new seeding system, a melted cesium as a seed material is pushed by a piston dozator, instead of gas-pressuredriven in the previous system. The main components such as the piston and the cesium store tank are consisted in a room where the temperature is maintained at constant $\sim 60^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The cesium pushed off is mixed with a heated argon gas at a pressure higher than that in the main hot duct, and introduced into the duct through a multi-hole nozzle.

The seed fraction was found to be more controllable in the new system and particularly the time response to the setting value was markedly improved, in comparison with the corresponding results in the previous system. Furthermore, the large fluctuation of seed fraction with a low frequency of several Hz observed in the previous system, which caused the fluctuation of output power as mentioned before, was diminished. This fact should be attributed to the smooth supply of seed material due to the mechanical piston motion. However, the discrepancy between the setting value and the seed fraction measured, which was also observed in the previous system, has remained. The discrepancy could be attributed to various reasons, for instance, a spatial nonuniformity of seed fraction, not complete vaporization of the seed material (droplet material), the flow in liquid phase along the duct surface, and so on. In order to clarify this fact, for the first time in the Fuji- 1 experiments, the spatial distribution of seed fraction in the downstream region from the injection nozzle in the hot duct has been roughly measured. The results will be shown in the following results of power generation experiments.

At high seed fractions, on the contrary, partially ionized seed plasma is considered to be dominant, because of low gas velocity and low Joule heating mentioned above. Consequently, at the optimum seed fraction, the fully ionized seed plasma dominates in the generator, resulting in large power output with its small fluctuation as well as that in the electron temperature.

Total pressures have been measured at different heights in the diffuser region in order to clarify the uniformity of fluid flow in the height direction (magnetic flux direction). Figure 8 shows the time variations of the total pressures measured at five different heights. During the seed injection ( $25 \sim 75 \mathrm{sec}$ ), the measured pressures almost coincide, whereas the pressures do not coincide at the period of no seed injection ( $<25 \mathrm{sec}$, and $>75 \mathrm{sec}$ ). This fact can imply that the uniform flow in the height direction is realized by MHD interaction. Thus, the stronger Lorentz force can be caused in the higher velocity part of fluid flow, which can make the flow uniform. Numerical simulations concerned with this fact should be needed.

As mentioned before, the spatial non-uniformity of seed fraction was observed. In order to improve the uniformity, the seed injection nozzle has been modified recently, where the diameters of 12 holes in the nozzle were identified accurately and the edge of each hole was dulled. The preliminary test of seed injection with the modified nozzle showed that the spatial non-uniformity of seed fraction in the downstream region from the injection nozzle in the hot duct was markedly improved. Thus, the seed fractions measured with the different three paths of transmitted lights almost coincided, in contrast with the seed fractions shown in Fig.3.

Another problem to overcome in order to improve the generator performance in the Fuji-1 facility is to reduce the impurity contamination in the working gas, particularly $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$, since the contamination will reduce the generator performance (Dellinger,T.C., and Gray, E.L.,1978, Yamasaki, H., et al., 1995). The fraction of $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$ roughly measured was several hundreds ppm, which was comparable to the seed fraction of the order of $10^{-4}$. This could be one of the reasons for the fact that the generator performance in the Fuji-1 blow-down facility is inferior to that in the shock-tube apparatus. To reduce the impurity contamination, and to clarify the acceptable level should be inevitable for the improvement of generator performance.

For the reduction of $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$ impurity contamination, the temperatures of ducts around the pebble-bed heat exchanger were carefully measured. Additional heaters were attached to the relatively low temperature regions. Furthermore, the heat-up operation of the heat exchanger was examined and the heat-up duration was prolonged. As a result, the $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$ impurity was confirmed to be reduced below 200 ppm , although the value
could not be sufficient for high power generation with high efficiency. After more efforts, the power generation experiment will be conducted.

## CONCLUSIONS

Recent experimental results on closed cycle MHD power generation with Fuji-1 facility were presented. In the experiments with a new disk generator (Disk-F4) and a new seed injection system, the maximum power output of 502 kW and the enthalpy extraction ratio of $16.7 \%$ have been obtained. These values at the present stage are lower than the maximum values previously achieved in this facility. However, the high power generation with the new generator is expected to be reliable.

The experimental results indicate that at the optimum seed fraction, the fully ionized seed plasma can dominate in the generator, resulting in large power output with its small fluctuation. Further, the static pressure increase at the second anode, which is considered to be ascribed to the Lorentz force in the nozzle region, was observed. Thus, these facts, which have been already pointed out in the power generation during several milli-seconds with shock tube facility, were also confirmed in the Fuji-1 blow-down experiments.
